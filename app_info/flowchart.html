Reading the flow, stage by stage

  1. Input. The teacher works through three wizard steps. The browser applies the same limits as the server (required fields, number ranges, lengths, and contradictions such as "1:1 devices but no devices"), so most mistakes are caught before anything is sent.
  2. Guardrails before any AI call. The server checks four things in a fixed order: the request is well-formed, the text is safe, the classroom details are consistent, and the topic is really in the chosen chapter. The first check that fails stops the request and sends a 422 with the exact field to fix. No AI time or quota is spent on a rejected request.
  3. Grounding. The topic is searched in the chosen chapter of the NCERT textbook, and the 4 closest passages become the only content the agents may teach. Their ids become the plan's citations.
  4. Agents, layer 1. CK sees only the passages, PK only the learners and the teaching method, and TK only the classroom setup. None of them sees another's output.
  5. Agents, layer 2. PCK, TCK and TPK each combine two layer-1 outputs and record the compromises they make, for example "inquiry scoped down because of time".
  6. Integration. TPACK reads all six outputs and the passages and writes the lesson plan. The plan must match the required format. If it doesn't, TPACK gets one more attempt with the list of problems.
  7. Guardrails after the AI. Citations to passages that weren't retrieved are removed, internal ids are stripped from the text, timing mismatches become warnings, content the textbook lacks is listed as "not covered", and any missing inputs become questions for the teacher.
  8. Stage 2. As soon as the plan is on screen, the browser asks for handouts and a quiz. This is a separate call, so if it fails the plan is still there and the teacher gets a Retry button.
  9. Afterwards. The teacher can open any citation to read the original textbook paragraph, answer a missing-detail question to regenerate, or print the teacher pack.

Guardrails: when each one fires

GuardrailWhereFires whenWhat happens
Browser checksindex.htmlA required field is empty, a number is out of range, text is too long, or the setup contradicts the devicesInline message under the field. Nothing is sent.
Well-formed requestapi.py (Pydantic)Missing or unknown field, invalid option, out-of-range value. Text is also cleaned (hidden characters removed, spacing fixed).422: the field is highlighted and the wizard jumps to its step
Safe textvalidation.pyA free-text field looks like an instruction to the AI ("ignore previous instructions") or contains clearly unsafe content422 with a neutral message. The matched words are never repeated.
Consistent classroomvalidation.pyDetails contradict each other, for example a computer lab with no devices422. A softer issue such as "tools but no internet" only becomes a warning.
Topic in chaptervalidation.py, after the searchEven the closest passage is too far from the topic (distance > 0.80)422 "not covered by this chapter"
AI reply readablellm.pyGemini is busy, rate-limited, unavailable, or returns something that isn't valid JSONWaits and retries, then tries the next model. If every model fails: 502 error card.
Agent answer completeagents.pyA layer-1 or layer-2 agent leaves out a required part of its answer502 error card
Plan / materials formatagents.py + lesson_schema.pyThe plan or quiz doesn't match the expected structure (for example a quiz answer that isn't one of its options)One retry with the problems listed. A second failure gives a 502.
Citations and idsrun.pyThe plan cites a passage it wasn't given, or chunk ids appear in the proseRemoved quietly and counted in the planning notes
Timingrun.pyThe phase minutes don't add up to the lesson lengthWarning at the top of the plan

How the information changes along the way

Point in the flowWhat the information looks like
WizardForm fields, each tagged with a name such as technology.student_devices
RequestNested JSON: content, learners, pedagogy, technology, other_requests
After the request checksA cleaned, typed request. Option codes such as 5E are turned into readable labels ("5E Model (Engage, …)") and unanswered optional fields become "Not specified".
After the search4 passages, each with an id like g7_ch01_s1.2_000, its section and its text
Layer 1 and 2 agentsJSON notes: concepts and misconceptions, a phase plan, a technology profile, then content per phase, visual per concept, tool per phase, and trade-offs
TPACKThe lesson plan: overview, phases (steps, board notes, questions, misconceptions, fallback, sources), not covered, design decisions
After the output checksThe same plan, cleaned, plus warnings, missing-detail questions and planning notes
Stage 2Handouts (tasks per phase) and an exit ticket (multiple choice with answer, explanation and Bloom's level)
Screen and printPlan, Handouts and Quiz tabs. The printed pack adds a student quiz and a separate answer key.
